# Vektor Terminal changelog

Short dated log of changes to `index.html` (the whole app) and why.

## 2026-11-16
- **Custom indicator scripts (Vektor Script).** A "Scripts" button on the chart opens an editor where you can write your own indicators in a small, purpose-built language (for example `macd = ema(close, 12) - ema(close, 26)` and `plot(macd, "MACD", color=blue)`), then add them to the Workspace/Supercharts chart.
  - **Safety:** scripts run in the app's own interpreter (its own lexer, parser and evaluator). They can't call `eval`/`Function`, reach page objects or storage, use member access (`a.b`), or loop without limit. Source size, statement count, nesting, look-back and total work are all capped.
  - **Language:** series (`open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, `hl2`, ...), `close[1]` for past values, arithmetic/comparison/logic and `?:`. Functions include sma/ema/rma/wma/rsi/stdev/atr/highest/lowest/roc/crossover. Declarations: `indicator()`, `input()`, `plot()` (line/histogram/area) and `hline()`. Colours can be names or hex.
  - **Errors:** shown with line and column, a plain-English hint and "Did you mean" suggestions for typos, plus a "Go to line" button. The chart legend marks scripts with errors.
  - **Chart:** overlay scripts draw on the price scale; others draw in their own stacked bands at the bottom. Inputs are editable in the editor, and the last value updates live with the feed.
  - **Examples:** MACD, RSI with bands, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic, EMA cross, Keltner channel and rate of change.
  - **Import/export:** `.vks.json` files. Imports are sanitised (only name, source and numeric inputs are kept), size-limited (512 KB) and always start off the chart.
  - **Storage:** scripts are saved with your other settings (`scr2`); the cookie/storage table now says so. Fixed while testing: Save showed no message, input changes rebuilt the field being edited, and the legend's error marker lagged one redraw.
- **Mobile QA pass (phones 390px and 360px, tablet 768px, touch).** Audited every page, dialog and new feature. There was no page-level sideways scrolling to fix; the fixes are in a new `src/96_touch.css`:
  - **Tap targets:** every control outside the chart canvas is now at least 44x44px on phones and tablets. This covers the watchlist "+ Symbol"/"+ Section"/edit/remove buttons, the chart header icons and dock buttons, the dock rows, Screener sort headers and rows, the calculator account table inputs and remove buttons, Settings colour swatches and tabs, dialog close buttons, the heatmap and mini-chart interval buttons, footer and legal-page links, the skip link, the VT home button and the sign-in "Show" password toggle. Links inside legal text get a taller hit area without moving the text.
  - **Tablet:** all eight pages are visible in the nav (it wraps instead of hiding Backtest off-screen); the chart is taller (up to 62% of the screen) and the drawing-tool column scrolls instead of leaving a large empty area; tablet controls use 44px instead of 38px.
  - **Phones:** the chart header (star, Indicators, Alerts, Settings) starts at the left edge instead of floating right; the Settings tab strip scrolls in one row instead of squeezing labels together.
  - The keyboard-shortcut overlay stays keyboard-only: its footer button is hidden on touch screens.
- **End-of-day session summary.** "Today" in the header (or More → Today’s session on phones) opens a summary of the day since 00:00 local time. From 21:00, a small chip offers it once a day.
  - **Data:** everything comes from data the app already has; nothing is estimated or filled in.
  - **P&L:** paper results of your long/short position-tool drawings. Positions closed today (target or stop hit, walked bar by bar with the existing position logic) and positions still open (valued at the live price) are shown, each with its own account and risk settings. They are clearly labelled as not broker trades.
  - **Markets:** up/down breadth and the top gainers and losers (live price against the previous daily close, the same as the heatmap and the "% change 1D" column), plus your watchlist's movers. It shows how many markets have daily candles.
  - **Alerts triggered today:** taken from the new alert log, with time, level, price and note.
  - Empty sections say so ("No positions closed today or open now", "No alerts triggered today"). With offline sample data, a warning says the prices are not real.
  - **Export:** "Copy as text" or "Download .txt". Clicking a mover opens its chart. The not-advice note is included.
  - Tests: new `tests/summary.js`.

- **Alert sound cue (off by default).** Triggered alerts used to always play a fixed beep. Now they make no sound unless you turn it on in the Price Alerts dialog ("Alert sound": on/off, volume, test).
  - The tone is a soft two-note chime made with Web Audio, with no files and nothing downloaded.
  - **Consent:** turning it on works straight away for the current tab. It is only remembered on the device (localStorage `vt_prefs`) if you allow the new **Optional preferences** category in Cookie settings. That category is off by default, on with "Accept all", and off with "Reject all". Withdrawing it deletes `vt_prefs`. If you turn the sound on first and allow the category later, the setting is saved then. Consent records made before this category existed count as "no".
  - The cookie notice, the storage table and the banner text describe the new category. The policy version is unchanged because the category is new, opt-in and off by default (flagged for solicitor review).
  - **Alert log:** each triggered alert is now logged with its time, level and price (the last 200, kept with your alerts). The session summary uses this log.
  - Tests: new `tests/sound.js`.

- **Mini-chart mode.** A new grid button next to "Data" in the screener list toolbar switches the list to a compact grid of small candle charts.
  - **What it shows:** the symbols on the current page of the list, in the same filter, sort and page order as the table, on Workspace and Screener.
  - **Each card:** the live price, the change over the bars shown, the last 48 candles with a dotted last-price line, and the window (for example "48 × 1H · ~2d").
  - **Interval:** 15m, 1H, 4H or 1D.
  - **Data:** comes from the existing candle store and live updates. Missing candles are queued through the normal loader, 8 at a time, and show a shimmering loading state. A card is only redrawn when its last bar or price changes, batched into one animation frame (render median about 4ms with the grid on).
  - Click or Enter opens the full chart, and each card has a full label for screen readers. The choice is remembered with your other settings. On phones the grid has two columns.
  - Tests: new `tests/minicharts.js`.

- **Keyboard shortcut overlay.** Press `?` anywhere (except while typing in a field, over another dialog, or on the sign-in screen) to see the keyboard shortcuts. There is also a "Keyboard shortcuts" button in the footer, hidden on touch-only phones.
  - The list covers only shortcuts the app really has: general keys, the screener list (Up/Down), the chart (intervals, symbol search, pan, undo/redo, save, Alt+drawing tools, hide drawings, delete, Esc) and the heatmap (arrows, Home/End, Enter).
  - The current page's sections come first and are marked "This page". On a Mac the keys show as ⌘ and ⌥.

  - **Tick flashes:** when a number changes in the screener table, the watchlist or the chart header price, its cell gets a short green or red tint that fades out. The table is rebuilt on every tick, so a running flash carries on with a negative delay instead of restarting. Flashes are found by comparing the DOM before and after the normal render; the feeds and sockets are untouched.

  - **Dialogs:** the backdrop fades and the dialog scales up slightly. On phones, dialogs slide up as a sheet with the same curve.

  - Tests: new `tests/motion.js`. Render takes a median of about 1.3ms per tick with flashes on.

- **Heatmaps page (was a placeholder).** A treemap of the screener's markets, built from the same data as the screener.
  - **Data:** tiles use the live price from the existing feeds against the previous candle close (1H, 4H, 1D or 1W). Colour can also show the screener's momentum read-out or the Vektor score, both computed with the existing unchanged scoring functions. - **Multi-page router and top nav (stage 1 of the multi-page build).** There's now a nav bar in the header: Workspace, Supercharts, Screener, Heatmaps, Calendar, Journal, Calculators, Backtest. Pages are shown and hidden in the same document, so the WebSockets (BNWS, BNKL, FHWS) and the LIVE candle cache are never closed or rebuilt; a test checks this. Workspace stays the default, and the VT logo still goes home. Supercharts shows the chart full width and Screener shows the table full width. The other pages are placeholders for now. Chart keyboard shortcuts now only work on pages with a chart, so typing elsewhere doesn't pop up the symbol search. Scoring and filter math is unchanged.
- **Reliability fixes (before the multi-page work).**
  - Alerts: `toast()` pointed at a `#msg` element that didn't exist, so every toast threw. `fireAlert` toasts first, so desktop notifications and the sound never ran. Added the toast element and made `toast` null-safe. Alerts now reuse one AudioContext (browsers cap how many you can open), get checked when polled (non-WebSocket) data arrives as well as on ticks, and show a "triggered" badge with a Re-arm button.
  - Live chart: each Binance ticker message (about once a second) rebuilt the whole chart when a crypto symbol was open. That reset your zoom and pan every second and started a new clock Web Worker each time without stopping the old one. Ticks now update the last bar and header price in place, and the clock worker is stopped on every redraw.
  - Universe: `persistUni()` took its snapshot before the newly loaded Binance/Finnhub/Polygon list was merged in. Because that snapshot is trusted for 6 hours, crypto (and key-based stock lists) could be missing from the screener. The merge now happens before the snapshot.
  - Defined the `--hover` colour that several menus use.
