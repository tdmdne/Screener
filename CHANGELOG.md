# Vektor Terminal changelog

Short dated log of changes to `index.html` (the whole app) and why.

## 2026-11-16
- **Phones (tested at 375x812 and 390x844 with touch emulation).**
  - **Layout:**
    - A fixed bottom tab bar with Workspace, Supercharts, Screener, Calculators and More. More opens a bottom sheet with Heatmaps, Calendar, Journal, Backtest, legal and privacy links, Cookie settings, the layout switch and the data status.
    - The header is compact, pages are single-column, and there's no horizontal page scroll on any page. The test checks this with the safety clip switched off.
    - Tables (`.ftbl`) scroll inside their own box, and the main controls are at least 44px tall.
  - **Fixed a baseline phone bug:** the phone card style for tables also restyled the chart library's internal layout table, which hid the price scale. It's now scoped out.
  - **Charts:** the drawing toolbar becomes a horizontal touch strip above a taller chart.
    - Gestures: one-finger horizontal pan, pinch to zoom, long-press for a crosshair that stays until the next tap, and vertical swipes scroll the page.
    - The price-scale "+" follows the crosshair on touch, so you can tap it to add an alert or a line.
    - Alert lines have bigger touch handles and can be dragged by finger. The alert editor and other dialogs open as bottom sheets above the tab bar.
  - **Drawing tools** now run only on pointer events. Before, the separate touch handlers made one tap count twice, which finished drawings at zero length on touch.
  - Reduced motion is respected app-wide. Tested in `tests/mobile.js` with Playwright `hasTouch`/`isMobile` and real CDP touch events. The test harness gained a `mobile` option.
- **UK GDPR and PECR mechanisms: consent banner, privacy centre and fuller legal pages** (template, so a solicitor still needs to review it).
  - The **storage consent banner** puts "Reject all" and "Accept all" on its first layer as identical buttons (same size, colour and weight), plus "Manage choices" with a toggle per category. The only optional category is the IndexedDB market-data cache, and it's off by default. Strictly necessary storage is shown as always on, and analytics or advertising as not used.
  - **No optional storage before consent.** `idb()` is gated, so the cache database isn't created until it's accepted, and it's deleted when consent is withdrawn.
  - Every choice is logged locally (`vt_consent_log`, last 50 entries) with a timestamp, method and policy version (`VT_POLICY_V`). Consent is re-asked after 12 months or when the version changes. A "Cookie settings" footer button reopens the choices at any time.
  - **Your data** (`#/legal/your-data`):
    - a live inventory of what this browser holds
    - a JSON export of all local data, covering access and portability, with the cache optional
    - "Delete all my data", which needs DELETE typed to confirm. It clears localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB and Cache Storage, disconnects the Binance and Finnhub sockets, then reloads.
    - consent status and history, with change and withdraw controls
    - a data subject request form (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability) that opens the user's own email app to the `[Contact email]` placeholder (`VT_DSR_EMAIL`, empty for now) and states the one-month response time. Its acknowledgement checkbox is unticked.
  - **Privacy Policy** now covers lawful bases per activity, retention periods, international transfers, all rights including the ICO complaint route, no automated decisions, and the 18+ rule.
  - **Third-party audit:** every API, WebSocket, proxy and CDN is listed with what each one receives. There are no web fonts or Google Fonts (system fonts only), icons are inline SVG, and no AI is used.
  - **New pages:** Refunds and Cancellation (for a future paid tier: prices up front, no hidden fees, cancel as easily as sign-up, the 14-day cooling-off period), About and Contact (imprint placeholders) and an Accessibility statement placeholder.
  - Terms state 18+ only, with no data knowingly collected from under-18s.
  - **Dark patterns:** "Keep me signed in" is no longer pre-ticked.
  - Legal pages also open in a dialog over the sign-in screen, and Esc closes it.
  - **N/A:** email unsubscribe (the app sends no email) and storage buckets (there's no backend).
  - Tested in `tests/privacy.js`. The test harness now seeds consent unless a test opts out.
- **Alerts drawn on the chart (TradingView-style), an add-on to the price-scale "+".** Every active, un-fired price alert for the open symbol is now a dashed orange line with a label on the price scale (bell icon plus price). This works on the Workspace chart and on both Supercharts panes.
  - Clicking the line or its label opens an alert editor where you can change the price, the condition (Crossing, Crossing up, Crossing down) or the pop-up message shown when it fires, or delete the alert. Dragging the line moves the price.
  - Edits change the same alert (same id) and persist. They update the Alerts dialog and the dock (both gained an edit link), and the line disappears once the alert fires.
  - Changing the price or condition re-arms the alert and resets the cross baseline to the current price, so an edit can't cause a duplicate or instant fire.
  - The alert engine gains a two-way "Crossing" condition, and a custom message replaces the default text in the toast and the notification. Existing alerts behave as before.
  - Tested headless in `tests/alertlines.js`: the line is drawn at the right price on all three charts, an edited message becomes the fired toast text, alerts fire exactly once, editing re-arms without a duplicate fire, dragging moves the price, delete removes both the line and the alert, and alerts survive a reload.
- **Legal pages (`#/legal/...`) and footer.** Adds Terms of Use (`#/legal/terms`), Privacy Policy (`privacy`, written for UK GDPR and the DPA 2018), Cookies and Local Storage (`cookies`), Risk Disclaimer and Not Financial Advice (`risk`), Data Sources and Attributions (`data`) and Disclaimer of Liability (`liability`), with `#/legal` as an index. The text describes what the code actually does: the exact localStorage, sessionStorage and IndexedDB keys; every market-data API, WebSocket, CORS proxy and CDN contacted; that the prototype sign-in stores only the username or email; and that API keys can pass through public proxies. There's no consent banner because the app sets no cookies and has no trackers, only strictly necessary storage. Company details are highlighted `[placeholders]`. A source comment marks the text as a template that needs a solicitor's review. A small footer carries a one-line risk warning and links to every page. Legal pages aren't nav tabs and aren't remembered as the last page. Opening the same page with a different sub-route re-renders it via a new `sub` router hook, and loading a deep link keeps its sub-route. Tested headless in `tests/legal.js`.
- **Price-scale plus button (TradingView-style), as the owner asked.** Hovering a chart or its price axis shows a small "+" next to the axis at the crosshair's price. Clicking it opens a menu with "Add alert at <price>" and "Draw horizontal line at <price>". The alert goes through the existing alert engine: it's saved, shows in the Alerts dialog and the Supercharts dock, fires once on the cross, and its direction comes from the current price. It works on the Workspace chart and on both Supercharts panes; a line added from pane 2 is drawn on the main pane, where drawings live. The old static "+" (which only switched to the h-line tool) is replaced. The existing Settings → Scales → "Plus button" checkbox now turns this on or off (on by default). Tested headless: alert price matches the "+" level, it persists, it fires exactly once on a mocked cross, and the h-line lands at the same price.
- **Calculators page (`#/calc`).** Account size defaults to the screener's `S.acct`, with risk per trade as % or a fixed $ amount. It covers:
  - Forex/metals position size: lots, units, pip value and risk at stop. Pip values for USD-quoted, USD-based and cross pairs use live prices, or a manual quote→USD rate when no live price is loaded.
  - Futures contracts, using exchange tick sizes and values for ES/MES, NQ/MNQ, YM/MYM, RTY/M2K, CL/MCL, NG, GC/MGC, SI, HG, ZN, ZB, 6E/6B/6J/6A, rounded down to stay within risk.
  - A pip and tick value converter and a drawdown recovery table (gain needed, and compounding wins needed).
  - Multiple prop accounts, showing lots and contracts per account plus totals.
  - A prop challenge simulator: Monte Carlo with a fixed seed, daily loss limit, static or trailing max DD, pass/fail/timeout rates, median days, expectancy and a balance path at expectancy.
  
  Inputs are saved. The maths lives in one pure `VTC` block covered by node unit tests (`tests/logic.js`).
- **Screener Pro page (`#/screener`).** Full-width table with no horizontal scrolling at 1600px (14 columns with the default layout). It adds six read-only columns: Market, 1D %, ADR 14d, Vektor score (`scoreMarket`), Expansion score and phase (`scoreSymbol`), and Tier, the strictest preset (P1 to P5) the market passes. Quick filter pills cover Market, Cycle (S01 to S04, which had filter logic but no buttons) and Rows (20/50/100). There's also a layout picker with Save/Export and a Columns button, which surface the existing saved-layout and column tools that had no buttons. Clicking a row (or using ↑/↓) opens a slide-over drawer with a mini chart (15m to 1W), the key scores, and buttons for Open in Supercharts, Workspace, watchlist and alert. Esc closes it. `render`/`openChart` are wrapped (not edited); the extra columns appear only on this page. The scoring functions are byte-identical to the baseline, and a test checks this.
- **Presets now load the candles they score on.** `scoreMarket`/`passesPreset` read an entry and a validation timeframe (H: 1H/1D, M: 15m/1H, L: 1m/15m). The loader only queued timeframes used by visible columns, so with a preset on, the entry timeframe often never loaded: Vektor scores were 0, P1 could not pass, and the P-sorting was meaningless. Those timeframes are now queued whenever P1 to P4 is active (or on Screener/Heatmaps), and missing scores show "…" instead of 0. The math is unchanged.
- **Supercharts page (`#/chart`).** The chart now fills the width (the screener list is hidden). New right-hand dock with Watchlist (plus the top screener rows), Active alerts (distance to price, hit/Re-arm, delete, + Alert) and an OHLC Data Window. The Data Window follows the crosshair and shows open, high, low, close, change, volume and EMA/SMA/RSI values. The dock collapses to a slim rail. There's a 1-pane / 2-pane toggle: pane 2 always shows the main chart's symbol on its own timeframe (5m to 1W, default 1D, for HTF/LTF work), uses your candle colours and EMA/SMA lines, and keeps its last bar live. The two panes share a crosshair (on by default, can be turned off). Pane count, pane-2 timeframe, dock and section states are saved. Pane 2 is torn down when you leave the page; the main chart and feeds are untouched. `drawChart` gets one new hook line (`vtAfterDraw`); everything else in it is unchanged.
- **Multi-page router and top nav (stage 1 of the multi-page build).** There's now a nav bar in the header: Workspace, Supercharts, Screener, Heatmaps, Calendar, Journal, Calculators, Backtest. Routing is hash-based (`#/workspace`, `#/chart`, `#/screener`, `#/heatmap`, `#/calendar`, `#/journal`, `#/calc`, `#/backtest`). The current page is kept in `S.pageView` and in localStorage (`vt_page`), so reloads and the Back button land where you were. Pages are shown and hidden in the same document, so the WebSockets (BNWS, BNKL, FHWS) and the LIVE candle cache are never closed or rebuilt; a test checks this. Workspace stays the default, and the VT logo still goes home. Supercharts shows the chart full width and Screener shows the table full width. The other pages are placeholders for now. Chart keyboard shortcuts now only work on pages with a chart, so typing elsewhere doesn't pop up the symbol search. Scoring and filter math is unchanged.
- **Reliability fixes (before the multi-page work).**
  - Alerts: `toast()` pointed at a `#msg` element that didn't exist, so every toast threw. `fireAlert` toasts first, so desktop notifications and the sound never ran. Added the toast element and made `toast` null-safe. Alerts now reuse one AudioContext (browsers cap how many you can open), get checked when polled (non-WebSocket) data arrives as well as on ticks, and show a "triggered" badge with a Re-arm button.
  - Live chart: each Binance ticker message (about once a second) rebuilt the whole chart when a crypto symbol was open. That reset your zoom and pan every second and started a new clock Web Worker each time without stopping the old one. Ticks now update the last bar and header price in place, and the clock worker is stopped on every redraw.
  - Universe: `persistUni()` took its snapshot before the newly loaded Binance/Finnhub/Polygon list was merged in. Because that snapshot is trusted for 6 hours, crypto (and key-based stock lists) could be missing from the screener. The merge now happens before the snapshot.
  - Defined the `--hover` colour that several menus use.
- **Baseline restored byte-for-byte.** `index.html` now matches the owner's attached baseline exactly. Line 361 (`esc` helper) had been mangled to `&amp;quot;` by an upload tool that decodes HTML entities; it is back to `&quot;`. All pushes now go through a method verified to round-trip entities exactly, and every push is checked byte-for-byte against the local file.
