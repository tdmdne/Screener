# Vektor Terminal

Vektor Terminal is a browser-based market screening and charting app. It shows prices, charts, a watchlist, alerts, a trade journal, calculators, heatmaps, an economic calendar and a bar-replay backtest lab.

It is a research tool that runs in your browser. It does **not** place trades, hold money, or connect to a brokerage account.

Scores, tiers (P1–P5), heatmaps, alerts, calculators and back-tests are rule-based calculations on price data. They do not predict prices and are not investment advice.
