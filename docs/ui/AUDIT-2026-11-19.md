## 2. Screen-by-Screen Findings (Severity: High / Medium)

### Global Chrome & Design System (`index.html:23-110, 324-332`)
- **[HIGH]** 2-row 93px desktop header (`#vnav` wraps below `1800px`) + 38px legal footer = ~131px permanent chrome tax.
- **[HIGH]** Raw diagnostic error text (`Loading 354 left · 1 failed (EURUSD: network error)`) and dev-style viewport switcher (`.mode-icons`) exposed in the top bar.
- **[HIGH]** Three clashing accent colors (orange `#f97316`, hardcoded `#2962ff` on `.preset-btn.on`, default browser-blue `<input type="range">`).
- **[HIGH]** Missing `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` and right-alignment across 60 numeric Screener cells & 34 Workspace cells.

### Supercharts (`index.html:160-162, 202-250, 368-401`)
- **[HIGH]** Two rows of chunky rounded pill buttons (`min-height: 38px`, `border-radius: 10px`) stack above the chart; on phone, ~560px of an 812px viewport is consumed by toolbars before candlesticks appear.
- **[HIGH]** Oversized dark boxed floating `EMA 10 / EMA 20` card occludes upper-left candles AND intercepts right-clicks (causing `tests/postool.js` failure).
- **[HIGH]** Live header price `#cpx` desyncs from chart close (`100.19` vs `129.74`) and fails `.vt-fu` tick flash (`tests/motion.js` failure).

### Screener (`index.html:110-128, 171-181, 462-483`)
- **[HIGH]** Three stacked control bars including a duplicate market selector (`CRYPTO` dropdown + `MARKET` pills).
- **[HIGH]** Phone shows 0 data rows above the fold (`6,507px` total scroll height) because all filters render expanded inline.
- **[MEDIUM]** Multi-line wrapped table headers (`~68px` tall) with misaligned gear icons; `P1–P5` buttons stretch vertically in Workspace left rail.

### Heatmap (`index.html:541-588`)
- **[HIGH]** Four stacked control bars (~195px chrome tax) above the treemap; 50% of `All` treemap renders dead gray `-` tiles with raw Yahoo codes (`6A=F`, `CL=F`, `BZ=F`).
- **[MEDIUM]** Phone `MARKET` bar clips mid-word (`Cryp`) and `TOP`/`BOTTOM` chips wrap chaotically.

### Backtest / Bar Replay (`src/54_backtest.css`, `src/54_backtest.js`)
- **[HIGH]** Permanent 145px Session Setup CRUD form stays expanded during active replay; chart locked to hardcoded `430px` height instead of full-height cockpit.
- **[HIGH]** Unstyled browser-default blue `<input type="range">` scrubber and form-like right rail — far below FX Replay standard.
- **[HIGH]** Hidden **656px** horizontal overflow on phone (`tests/mobile.js` failure) and 0px chart visible above the fold on phone.

### Journal & Prop Tracker (`src/51_journal.css`, `src/51_journal.js`)
- **[HIGH]** Flat 9-card identical KPI wall lacks hierarchy; 4 native `<select>` dropdowns in a heavy card waste ~90px even with zero trades.
- **[HIGH]** Dead black `"No closed trades yet"` void with defensive legal subtitle copy and zero guided empty-state CTAs.

## 3. Prioritized Remediation Roadmap
1. Single 48px institutional command bar + slim status footer + cohesive obsidian surface tokens (`--bg-0/1/2`, hairline borders, global `tabular-nums`, Apple cubic-bezier micro-interactions).
2. Supercharts single-row 40px pro toolbar + transparent inline single-line indicator legend + `#cpx` tick sync/flash fix + phone bottom-sheet overflow drawer.
3. Backtest / Replay overhaul into FX Replay–grade chart-first cockpit (collapsed session pill, custom scrubber, full-height canvas, zero phone overflow).
4. Screener unified 1-row toolbar (deduped market switcher, collapsible filter drawer on phone, 1-line headers, right-aligned `tabular-nums`, cohesive accent).
5. Journal executive analytics hierarchy (hero KPI strip + guided empty state + sleek filter pills) & Heatmap 2-bar header + human instrument names + hide empty tiles toggle.
6. Eliminate all non-origin third-party egress and wire thin display-only `dataService.js` to `/api/bootstrap` + `/ws`.
