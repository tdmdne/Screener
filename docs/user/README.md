# Vektor Terminal user guides

These pages describe what the running app does today. They are written from the code in `index.html` (the page the site actually serves). They are not a product roadmap.

Vektor Terminal is a browser tool for screening and charting markets. It does not place trades or connect to a broker. Scores, tiers and similar read-outs are calculations on past prices, not investment advice. The in-app footer links to the risk disclaimer and other legal pages.

## Guides

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) — sign-in and guest mode, the storage banner, layout, your first chart, the watchlist and screener, and API keys
- [Workspace and charts](workspace-charts.md) — Workspace, Supercharts, drawing tools, shortcuts, snapshots, themes and layouts
- [Indicators](indicators.md) — the built-in EMA, SMA, Bollinger Bands, Volume, RSI and ATR
- [Vektor Script](vektor-script.md) — the custom indicator language: syntax, functions, limits and examples
- [Screener](screener.md) — the market list and P1–P5 presets
- [Heatmaps](heatmaps.md) — the treemap of % change, momentum or Vektor score
- [Watchlists](watchlists.md) — your own symbol lists and sections
- [Alerts](alerts.md) — price alerts, notifications and the optional sound
- [Journal](journal.md) — trade log, prop-challenge tracker and optional Journal AI review
- [Calendar and news](calendar-and-news.md) — this week’s economic calendar and headlines
- [Calculators](calculators.md) — the seven position-size and prop-account calculators
- [Backtest and replay](backtest-and-replay.md) — manual bar-replay paper trading
