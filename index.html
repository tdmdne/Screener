My Screener

Prototype · sample data

In sample mode, values are simulated. Absolute = |latest close − close N candles ago|. Expansion = move from the lowest low (bullish) or highest high (bearish) of the last N candles to the latest close. Dollar volume = price × 7-day average volume × contract size; FX/CFD volume is tick volume, so it isn’t filtered. Tap a symbol to open its chart. Presets are saved in this browser only; use Export/Import to move them between devices.
